Hosting?

  1. Add your bars: a clue that leads to each one, and the answer teams enter when they arrive.
  2. Pick how many teams and the order each team visits the bars, with every team starting somewhere different.
  3. Send each team its link. After their route, everyone gets one final clue to the last bar.
